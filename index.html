About Us

Welcome to Travel Bug North East

At Travel Bug, we craft unforgettable journeys through the breathtaking landscapes of North East. With years of experience and a deep understanding of North East's culture and landscapes, we craft unforgettable journeys that showcase the region's natural beauty, rich heritage and warm hospitality.

Founded by passionate travellers, Travel Bug North East is committed to providing exceptional travel experiences that leave a lasting impression. Our team of local experts and guides ensure that every trip is tailored to your interests and preferences.

Our Mission
