About Comfort Travels

Comfort Travels is a premier travel agency located in Police Bazar, near Sardar Police Station, Shillong. We specialize in comprehensive travel services including tour guide arrangements, hotel booking, safari tours, and reliable transportation solutions. Known for our experienced local guides, comfortable accommodations, and exciting wildlife experiences, we prioritize safety, comfort, and authentic cultural immersion. We are ideal for tourists and groups looking for complete travel packages across all seven northeastern states of India.
